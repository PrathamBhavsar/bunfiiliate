# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Developers and small teams who run their own infrastructure and want affiliate/referral tracking they own: indie SaaS founders, platform engineers, finance-minded operators. They arrive from GitHub, a search, or a recommendation, on a laptop, wanting proof it is real before they install.

## Product Purpose
bunfiiliate is an open-source (AGPL-3.0) affiliate tracking and commission engine in Rust. Sub-millisecond click tracker with a crash-safe write-ahead spool, an append-only double-entry ledger in integer cents, multi-tenant programs, Stripe/Paddle/generic webhooks, payouts with approval and CSV. Self-host on one VPS.

## Brand Personality
Exact, unhurried, a little dry. Pixel-grid precision (the money adds up to zero). Confident through specifics, not adjectives.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, stock "growth" imagery, fake logos, invented customer counts, hero-metric tiles, icon-card grids.

## Design Principles
1. Prove with the mechanism: the hero shows clicks landing in a ledger that sums to zero.
2. Real numbers only: GitHub stars, downloads, benchmark results, or nothing. Never an invented claim.
3. Install in one copy-paste, docs one click away.
4. Dark only. One saturated field per page (the install band) carries the brand color at scale.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, keyboard-operable tabs and docs nav, `prefers-reduced-motion` freezes the ASCII canvas, canvas is aria-hidden with all copy in real text.

## Brand commitments
Fonts: Geist Pixel Square (display), Satoshi (body), Geist Mono (code). Icons: Hugeicons. Dark theme only. ASCII canvas hero background.
